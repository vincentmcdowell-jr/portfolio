Autonomous Systems Lab, fall 2025

Uno32 pin tester

An independent research project: a rig that checks every pin on a PIC32/Uno32 board and shows results live.

Hover or tap to see the constraint and the impact

Constraint

The board’s own self-test missed pin faults, and the Arduino tester lacked both the channels and the input compatibility to read the Uno32 directly.

Impact

Non-blocking state machines on both boards, a UART handshake feeding a Tkinter GUI, three 16-channel multiplexers (48 channels) with voltage-divider PWM mapping, and a custom PCB shield. Detection accuracy above the self-test [by X%]; setup cut from [X] to [X] minutes.

C, Python (Tkinter), UART, PCB design
