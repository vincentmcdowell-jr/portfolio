Who I am

About me

What first drew me to robotics was the idea of designing and building a system that makes people’s lives easier. I wanted to understand both how a robot functions, the hardware side, and how it thinks, the software side.

My strength is hardware conceptualization: turning an idea into a circuit design, implementing it, and proving it works through test analysis. I’ve done that as a project lead on my own research and inside cross-functional teams at Apptronik, Cummins, and Amazon Lab126, bringing new ideas and technical concepts to hard problems.

Leadership and community: Eagle Scouts mentor (current), San Jose Vietnamese Running Club (SJVRC) volunteer (current), and resident assistant at UC Santa Cruz (3 years).

Member of NSBE, IEEE, ACM, Google Developer Student Club, SASE, Santa Cruz Artificial Intelligence (SCAI), MESA/MEP, and the Black Men’s Initiative.
